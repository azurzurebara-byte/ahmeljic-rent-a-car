Use your built-in image generation tool to create ONE background image and save it as
assets-src/codex/fleet-bg.png (in the current working directory). Do not edit any other file.

What it is for: a backdrop behind transparent PNG cut-outs of real cars on small product cards of a car-rental
website (cards are 16:10, about 300 x 190 px on desktop and 160 x 100 px on phones). The car is placed on top
by the website, centered, filling about 85% of the width, with its wheels at about 80% of the height.

Image:
- Landscape 16:10, at least 1600 x 1000 px, no text, no logos, no cars, no people, no watermark.
- A clean, softly lit photo studio / showroom corner: a smooth light warm-grey floor (around #ece8df) meeting a
  slightly darker back wall at about 62% of the height, with a soft gradient and a gentle light pool in the
  middle of the floor where a car would stand. Very subtle; it must stay quiet so the car is the subject.
- Brand hint only at the very edges: a thin warm red (#c91420) and golden yellow (#f2bf00) accent, e.g. a soft
  out-of-focus red-to-yellow glow low on the back wall at the left and right edges. No hard stripes.
- Even, matte, low contrast in the centre band (35%-90% height) so dark, red, white and silver cars all read well.
- Photorealistic, not illustrated.

When done, print the saved path and the pixel size.
